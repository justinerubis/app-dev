# 🏎️ Ford v Ferrari (2019)

## 🎬 About the Movie
**Ford v Ferrari** is an American sports drama film released in 2019, directed by James Mangold. It stars Matt Damon and Christian Bale.

## 📖 Movie Synopsis
The movie tells the true story of American car designer **Carroll Shelby** and British race car driver **Ken Miles**. Together, they build a powerful racing car for Ford to compete against Ferrari in the 24 Hours of Le Mans in 1966.

## ⭐ My Favorite Characters
- **Ken Miles** – A talented and passionate race car driver.
- **Carroll Shelby** – A brilliant car designer.
- **Henry Ford II** – The leader of Ford Motor Company.

## ❤️ Why I Like This Movie
I like *Ford v Ferrari* because it shows the importance of teamwork, courage, passion, and never giving up on your dreams.

My favorite part is the exciting racing scenes and the competition between Ford and Ferrari.
